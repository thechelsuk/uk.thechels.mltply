Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the Mltply app really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no sign-up. The code is open source on GitHub.

What do the printable workbooks cover?

Times tables 2 to 12 and division, addition and subtraction, fractions, squares, cubes and roots, and KS2 SATs arithmetic practice papers, for Years 1 to 7 following England's national curriculum.

Do the workbooks include answers?

Yes. Every answer is worked out and checked by computer, so marking takes minutes.

Where can I buy the workbooks?

As instant PDF downloads on Gumroad and Etsy. On Etsy, single workbooks start from £4.79 and the Years 1 to 7 bundle is £17.99.

Does my child's data leave the device?

No. Progress stays on your child's device and nothing is shared.
